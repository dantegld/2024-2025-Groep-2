Importance Of The Unique Text


When registering, we ask you to enter a unique text. This text is essential in case you forget your password. When you request to reset your password, you must have this text on hand to complete the verification. You must remember this text because you will need it to reset your password.

Note: The unique text cannot be changed later, so make sure you remember it well. This helps us keep your account secure and ensures that only you have access to your data.
